Basically I wanted something to help me work out how long I would want my hair as I grow it out from a Buzz.

Open the HTML in your browser, preferably on a desktop
upload a collection of photos of hair at different lengths
follow the prompts to describe the hair length in the photos
then switch to the map tab 
adjust the sliders so everything fits nicely on the graph
Now you can view the different hair cut options as your hair grows out
You can export
You can view the different categories for the hair you have described in the families tab
